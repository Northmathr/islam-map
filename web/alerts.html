Planning alerts

An email when a mosque planning application appears in your council

← Back to the map

Planning applications are public records. This sends you an email when one relating to a mosque appears in the register for a council you choose, with a link to the council’s own record.

Read the volume before you sign up: there were 170 such applications across the whole United Kingdom in 2026. For most councils that is a few a year, and for many it is none. This is not a newsletter and there is no schedule — quiet months are the normal case.

None chosen yet.

    What you will and will not get

    • One email per council, when something appears. Not a digest on a schedule.
    • Only applications matched in their description, not ones that merely mention a mosque in the address as a landmark. That excludes 226 of the 1,668 applications on record, and it is why you will occasionally hear nothing about something you spot yourself.
    • Nothing about conditions and amendments. Those are paperwork attached to a permission that already exists, and they read as new applications when they are not.
    • Nothing older than 60 days, even when it is new to this project. The source backfills: its 2023 figure is 248 against roughly 50 a year either side, which is record-keeping catching up rather than a spike in activity. Without that cut-off one backfill would mail you about applications from years ago.

    Coverage is uneven, and silence is not proof

    The planning source aggregates local authority registers unevenly. Of the 1,668 applications on record, 1,565 are English, 65 Scottish, 37 Welsh and one Northern Irish. If you subscribe to a council outside England you may hear nothing for a long time, and that is at least partly the record rather than the activity. Check the council’s own planning portal if something matters to you.

    An alert means an application exists in the public register. It is not a decision, and an approval is not a building. The sources and method page sets out what each source can and cannot show.
